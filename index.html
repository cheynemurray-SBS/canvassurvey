Section One

Why We're Asking

4–6
years between full reviews A learning management system is one of the most consequential tools we provide. On that rhythm, we step back from day-to-day support tickets and ask a bigger question: does the platform still fit how the university teaches?

IT can see uptime, login counts, and support volume, but we can't see directly whether Canvas is actually shaping a good course learning experience. That's only visible from inside the classroom. A grading workflow that feels clunky, a feature nobody uses, a gap between what the system offers and what a course actually needs: these are easy to miss from the help desk queue alone. A periodic review is how we go looking for them on purpose.

Faculty know where the platform helps and where it gets in the way in a way that no usage report can capture. This survey is how that perspective reaches us in a form we can act on.

Section Two

What We're Asking

Nine short questions, about five to seven minutes, covering the specifics of how Canvas fits your teaching:

Course Content & Design

How well Canvas supports building and organizing your course.

Gradebook

Recording and communicating grades to students.

Communication Tools

Announcements, Inbox, and Discussions for reaching students.

Integrations

Publisher content, Microsoft Education, and the tools you rely on.

Reliability

How often outages or performance issues disrupt teaching.

Overall Fit

Whether Canvas meets the needs of your teaching and course design, and what would concern you about a migration.
